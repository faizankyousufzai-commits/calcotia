Why these calculators exist

Almost every question about pay at work ends in a rulebook. How much you receive when you are sick, what your holiday pay is worth this year, what a pension comes to after forty years of work: almost none of it is decided by your employer alone. It is set by labour law, social insurance rules and tax law, and the official pages that explain it are written for payroll specialists, not for the person waiting for the money.

Calcotia starts from those same official rules and turns them into a number you can check. Every calculator shows the full breakdown behind its result, names the rates it used and the year they belong to, and links the official source at the bottom of the page, so the answer is never just a number you have to take on trust.

Questions people ask about Calcotia

Is Calcotia really free?

Yes. Every calculator on this site is free to use, with no account, no sign up and no paywall. The site is supported by advertising, never by charging the people who come here for an answer.

Are these the official government calculators?

Usually the official bodies do not publish a simple calculator at all, only the law and long guidance pages. Ours are independent calculators that implement the official formulas and current rates, and each page links its official sources so you can verify the rule yourself.

Why is every country section in a different language?

Because these entitlements are national. A holiday pay rule in Norway only exists in Norwegian law, and the people it affects search for it in Norwegian. Writing each section in the local language keeps the legal terms exact and the page findable for the people who need it.

How accurate is the result?

The calculators follow the standard rules and the rates for the year shown on the page, and they are tested against worked examples before they go live. Individual cases can still differ, for example under a collective agreement or a special scheme, and where a calculator simplifies a rule, the page says so.

Do you store the numbers I type in?

No. Every calculation runs entirely in your own browser. Your salary, dates and other figures never leave your device and are not saved anywhere.
