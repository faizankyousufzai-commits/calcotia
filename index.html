About Calcotia

Calcotia exists for one simple reason: the money questions people ask at work, like how much sick pay, holiday pay, severance or pension they will actually get, are answered by complicated official rules in every country, yet most people just need a clear number with the working shown. We build one careful calculator per country, in that country's own language, using the official formula and current rates, and we show every step so you can check the result yourself.
