Last updated 2026-11-09 · By Faizan Khan Yousufzai

Our free brick calculator turns a wall size into the order your supplier understands: bricks, mortar bags and cost. Enter the wall length and height, take off any doors and windows, and it works out how many bricks you need in seconds. It uses the standard modular brick with 3/8 inch joints, and it adds a waste allowance for cuts and breakage.

How to Use Calcotia's Brick Calculator

  1. Measure the wall length and height in feet.
  2. Enter the area of any doors and windows in square feet, or leave it at zero.
  3. Pick your brick type: standard modular brick is the default, and king-size brick covers more area per brick.
  4. Choose single or double thickness. A double wall needs twice the bricks.
  5. Set the waste allowance; 10 percent covers most walls.
  6. Click Calculate and read your brick count, mortar bags and cost.

Why Use This Tool

  • Built on the real brick. The standard US modular brick is 7-5/8 by 3-5/8 by 2-1/4 inches, giving 6.75 bricks per square foot with 3/8 inch joints.
  • Openings taken off. How to calculate bricks for wall jobs means counting the net area, after subtracting doors and windows.
  • Waste included. Cuts, breakage in delivery and faulty bricks eat into your supply, so the calculator adds your waste allowance.
  • Mortar counted too. The calculator counts 80 lb bags of mortar mix, about 7 bags per 100 square feet for a single wall.
  • Cost at a glance. Enter a price per brick for the material cost, the fast answer to how many bricks do i need calculator and calculator bricks searches.

Who Can Use It

  • Homeowners. Price a garden wall or a brick mailbox before you call the yard.
  • DIY builders. Work out how to calculate bricks in a wall for a weekend project without over-ordering.
  • Masons. Quote a job on site with a brick wall calculator figure, mortar bags and cost in one pass.
  • Contractors. Check a calculation brick wall estimate, or any calculator brick wall figure, against the plan before ordering a full pallet.

Tips for Best Results

  • Measure the full length and height, including any part below ground level.
  • Use 5 percent waste for a plain wall, 10 percent for a typical wall, 15 percent for arches and angles.
  • Order all bricks from the same batch: colour varies between batches, and a patchwork wall shows it.
  • Keep the mortar joint at 3/8 inch: thicker joints use more mortar and change the count.
  • A double wall needs twice the bricks and twice the mortar of a single wall.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many bricks per square foot?

About 6.75 standard modular bricks per square foot, with 3/8 inch joints in a single layer. King-size bricks need only about 4.5.

How to calculate bricks in wall area with a door?

Work out length x height, then subtract the door and window areas. A 20 by 10 foot wall less a 3 by 7 foot door leaves 179 square feet.

How many bricks do I need for a 100 square foot wall?

About 675 modular bricks before waste, or about 743 with a 10 percent allowance. The calculator shows the same answer, plus mortar bags.

How much mortar do I need for a brick wall?

About 7 bags of 80 lb mortar mix per 100 square feet of single wall. A bigger wall or a double wall scales the mortar with it.
