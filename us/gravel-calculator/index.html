Gravel Calculator: How Much Gravel Do You Need?

Last updated 2026-11-09 · By Faizan Khan Yousufzai

Our free gravel calculator turns the size of your area into the order your supplier understands: cubic yards, tons and 50 lb bags. Enter the length, width and depth, pick pea gravel or crushed stone, and it works out how much gravel you need in seconds. It suits driveways, paths, patios, garden beds and even fish tanks.

How to Use Calcotia's Gravel Calculator

  1. Measure the length and width of the area in feet.
  2. Pick a depth in inches: paths and beds usually need 2 to 3 inches, driveways need 4 to 6 inches.
  3. Choose pea gravel or crushed stone for the weight in tons.
  4. Set the waste allowance: 10 percent covers most jobs, 25 percent suits a driveway that will compact.
  5. Click Calculate and read your cubic yards, tons and bag count.

Why Use This Tool

  • Three answers at once. Suppliers sell by the yard, some quote by the ton, and small jobs come in bags.
  • How to calculate how much gravel i need: cubic feet = length x width x depth in feet, then cubic yards = cubic feet / 27.
  • The right depth for the job. A gravel driveway calculator must allow for depth: driveways need 4 to 6 inches, while a path needs only 2 to 3.
  • Pea gravel covered. A pea gravel calculator uses 1.35 tons per cubic yard, the dry weight of pea gravel.
  • Fish tanks too. A gravel calculator fish tank job is a small rectangle, so enter the tank base and the substrate depth.

Who Can Use It

  • Homeowners. Price a new path, patio or driveway before you call a supplier.
  • Landscapers. Quote a driveway gravel calculator figure on site and order the right tonnage the first time.
  • Gardeners. Top up beds and borders without buying a full yard when half a yard will do.
  • Aquarium keepers. Work out substrate for a new tank setup from the tank footprint.

Tips for Best Results

  • Measure at the widest points: curves and slopes hide extra square feet.
  • Keep the depth honest: 2 inches for a walkway, 3 inches for a dog run or play area.
  • For a driveway, lay a compacted crushed stone base first and keep any pea gravel top layer thin.
  • Add 10 percent for waste on flat jobs and 25 percent where gravel will be compacted.
  • One cubic yard covers 162 square feet at 2 inches, 108 at 3 inches and 81 at 4 inches.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to calculate how much gravel you need?

Multiply length x width x (depth / 12) to get cubic feet, then divide by 27 for cubic yards. That is how to calculate yards of gravel for any rectangle.

How much gravel do i need calculator for a driveway?

A driveway needs 4 to 6 inches over a compacted base. Enter the size with 4 inches of depth and 25 percent waste for yards, tons and bags.

How much does one cubic yard of gravel weigh?

Pea gravel weighs about 2,700 lb, or 1.35 US tons. Crushed stone is a little heavier, about 1.40 tons. About 54 bags of 0.5 cubic feet make one cubic yard.

Can I use this gravel calculator for a fish tank?

Yes. Enter the inside length and width of the tank and the depth of gravel you want. The bag count tells you how many 50 lb bags to buy.
