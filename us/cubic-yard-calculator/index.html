Cubic Yard Calculator: How Many Yards Do You Need?

Last updated 2026-11-09 · By Faizan Khan Yousufzai

Our free cubic yard calculator turns three measurements into the number every supplier asks for: cubic yards. Enter the length and width of your area and the depth of the material to get cubic feet, cubic yards and an order quantity with waste included. It suits soil, gravel, mulch and concrete.

How to Use Calcotia's Cubic Yard Calculator

  1. Measure the length and width of the area in feet.
  2. Decide the depth in inches: a garden bed is often 3 to 6 inches, a concrete slab often 4 inches.
  3. Enter the three numbers and pick a material for a weight in tons.
  4. Set the waste allowance; 10 percent covers most jobs.
  5. Click Calculate and read your cubic yards, cubic feet and order quantity.

Why Use This Tool

  • One yard is 27 cubic feet. A cubic yard is a cube 3 feet on each side, so 3 x 3 x 3 = 27.
  • How to calculate cubic yards? Multiply length by width by depth in feet, then divide by 27.
  • How to calculate cubic yards from square feet: multiply the area by the depth in feet, then divide by 27.
  • Dirt uses the same maths. A cubic yard calculator dirt estimate divides the same three measurements by 27, because dirt is sold by the cubic yard.
  • Concrete too. How to calculate cubic yards of concrete is the same formula. A 10 by 12 foot slab, 4 inches thick, is 1.48 cubic yards.

Who Can Use It

  • Homeowners. Work out soil for a raised bed or gravel for a path before you call the yard.
  • Landscapers. Run a cubic yards calculator check on every quote, instead of guessing from a truckload.
  • DIY builders. Size a concrete slab or a mulch order without a second trip to the store.
  • Contractors. A cubic yard estimator like this one keeps orders and invoices consistent across a crew.

Tips for Best Results

  • Measure at the widest points, and use the largest depth if the ground is uneven.
  • Convert inches to feet first by dividing by 12: a 4 inch depth is 0.333 feet.
  • Round up, never down: suppliers often sell in whole or half yards.
  • Keep waste at 10 percent for flat beds and raise it for rough or sloped ground.
  • A cubic yardage calculator gives volume, not weight: wet soil weighs far more than dry soil.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate cubic yards?

Multiply length by width by depth, all in feet, to get cubic feet, then divide by 27.

How to calculate cubic yards from square feet?

Multiply the area in square feet by the depth in feet, then divide by 27. A 200 square foot bed at 3 inches deep is 1.85 cubic yards.

How to calculate cubic yards of concrete?

Use the same formula with the slab length, width and thickness. A 4 inch slab is 0.333 feet thick, so add about 10 percent extra.

How many cubic feet are in a cubic yard?

27. A yard is 3 feet long, so a cube one yard on each side holds 3 x 3 x 3 = 27 cubic feet.
