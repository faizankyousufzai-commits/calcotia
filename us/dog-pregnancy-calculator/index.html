How to Use Calcotia's Dog Pregnancy Calculator

Why Use This Tool

  • A window, not one day. Dogs rarely deliver on day 63 exactly, so you see a range.
  • Week-by-week view. Follow your dog pregnancy calculator week by week.
  • Plain-language milestones. Ultrasound, whelping-box setup, and temperature checks are explained simply.
  • Easy input. This pregnancy in dogs calculator needs only a date. Gestation length is similar across breeds.
  • Honest estimates. Results are a planning guide. Your vet confirms pregnancy and timing.

Who Can Use It

  • First-time owners. After a surprise mating, run a quick calculator dog pregnancy check to see when puppies may arrive. Then call your vet.
  • Breeders. Plan vet visits, helpers, and time off around the whelping window.
  • Foster volunteers. Mating date unknown? A vet can estimate gestational age by ultrasound.

Tips for Best Results

  • Use the mating date, not the day you noticed symptoms or got a scan.
  • Treat any pregnancy calculator dog result as a window to plan around, not a promise.
  • Book an ultrasound for around day 25 to 28.
  • Set up the whelping box one to two weeks early in a warm, quiet spot.
  • From about day 55, take her temperature twice daily and write it down. A drop below 99°F often means labor within 12 to 24 hours.
  • Keep your vet's emergency number near the whelping box.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is a dog pregnant?

Dog pregnancy lasts about 63 days from ovulation. Counted from mating, normal births fall between days 58 and 68, because sperm can survive for days before the eggs are ready. That is why you see a range.

How does a dog pregnancy due date calculator handle several matings?

Enter the first mating date for the earliest likely window. You can also run the last date and watch the combined range. Ovulation sets the real clock, so a vet's progesterone test gives the tightest estimate.

When can a vet confirm my dog is pregnant?

An ultrasound can confirm pregnancy around day 25 to 28. A vet blood test for relaxin works in a similar window. No reliable at-home or urine test exists for dogs. An X-ray later in pregnancy counts the puppies.

What if my dog goes past her due date?

Call your vet if labor has not started well after day 68. Also call right away for green discharge before a puppy arrives, weakness, or hard straining for 30 to 60 minutes with no puppy. Whelping problems can turn serious fast.
