Aquarium Calculator: Find Your Tank Volume in Gallons

Last updated 2026-11-09 · By Faizan Khan Yousufzai

Our free aquarium calculator turns your tank measurements into gallons and litres in seconds. Enter the length, width, and height of a rectangular tank, or the diameter and height of a cylinder tank, and get the volume in US gallons, UK gallons, and litres. It also shows the real water volume once gravel and decorations take up space.

How to Use Calcotia's Aquarium Calculator

  1. Pick your tank shape: rectangular or cylinder.
  2. Pick your units: inches or centimetres.
  3. Enter the inside length, width, and water depth. For a cylinder, enter the diameter and the water depth instead.
  4. Click Calculate to see gallons and litres.

How to calculate aquarium volume: multiply length by width by height, then divide by 231 for US gallons. This aquarium volume calculator does it in both inches and centimetres.

Why Use This Tool

  • Exact formula. One US gallon is exactly 231 cubic inches, so this volume of aquarium calculator uses the real conversion.
  • Both shapes. Rectangular tanks use length times width times height; cylinder tanks use pi times radius squared times height.
  • Gallons and litres. Get US gallons, UK gallons, and litres together, so dosing guides and stocking charts all match.
  • Real water volume. A tank is never full to the rim, and gravel displaces water. The tool shows the realistic number too.

Who Can Use It

  • New fish keepers. Before buying fish, use this aquarium gallon calculator to find out what your tank really holds.
  • Tank shoppers. An aquarium size calculator check tells you whether a second-hand tank is really the size claimed.

Tips for Best Results

  • Measure inside the glass, not outside. The frame and glass thickness can add an inch or more to each side.
  • Use the water depth, not the full tank height. Most tanks run an inch or two below the rim.
  • Subtract about 10 percent for gravel, rocks, and decorations when you dose medicine or plan stocking.
  • Check the label after measuring. A 24 by 12 by 16 inch tank calculates to about 19.9 gallons and is sold as a 20-gallon tank, which is what any fish tank calculator should show.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to calculate aquarium gallons?

Measure the inside length, width, and water depth in inches, multiply them, then divide by 231. A tank that is 24 by 12 by 16 inches holds 4,608 cubic inches, and 4,608 divided by 231 is about 19.9 US gallons.

How to calculate gallons in a aquarium?

In inches, divide cubic inches by 231 for US gallons. In centimetres, divide cubic centimetres by 1,000 for litres, then divide litres by 3.785 for US gallons. For a cylinder tank, use pi times radius squared times height, then divide by 231.

How to calculate aquarium volume for a round tank?

Measure the diameter and water height, halve the diameter for the radius, then use pi times radius squared times height. Divide the cubic inches by 231 for US gallons. A cylinder 24 inches across and 20 inches deep holds about 39 gallons.

What is the difference between US gallons and UK gallons?

A US gallon is 3.785 litres and a UK (imperial) gallon is 4.546 litres, about 20 percent larger. A 55 US-gallon tank holds about 45.8 UK gallons. This calculator shows both, so check which gallon your guide means.
