Topsoil Calculator: How Much Topsoil Do You Need?

Last updated 2026-11-09 · By Faizan Khan Yousufzai

Our free topsoil calculator turns the size of your bed or lawn into the order your supplier understands: cubic yards, tons and bags. Enter the length, width and depth, and it works out how much topsoil you need in seconds. It suits garden beds, lawns, levelling and raised beds, and it adds a waste allowance so settling does not leave you short.

How to Use Calcotia's Topsoil Calculator

  1. Measure the length and width of the area in feet.
  2. Pick a depth in inches: lawn topdressing needs 1 to 2 inches, a new lawn needs 4 to 6 inches, garden beds need 6 to 8 inches.
  3. Enter the three numbers and choose your bag size if you are buying bags.
  4. Set the waste allowance; 10 percent covers most jobs.
  5. Click Calculate and read your cubic yards, tons and bag count.

Why Use This Tool

  • Three answers at once. Bulk soil is sold by the yard, some quote by the ton, and small jobs come in bags. This online topsoil calculator gives you all three.
  • How to calculate how much topsoil i need: cubic feet = length x width x depth in feet, then cubic yards = cubic feet / 27.
  • Depth matters. A topsoil calculator yards answer is only as good as the depth. Too thin and roots starve; too thick and you overpay.
  • Weight included. Dry screened topsoil weighs about 2,100 lb per cubic yard, or about 1.05 tons. Wet soil weighs more.
  • Bags counted. A topsoil calculator in yards also counts bags, so you know how many to load into the car.

Who Can Use It

  • Homeowners. Top up a lawn, level a bumpy patch or fill a new bed without over-ordering.
  • Gardeners. Work out soil for vegetable beds and raised beds before the planting weekend.
  • Landscapers. Quote a how much topsoil do i need calculator figure on site and order the right tonnage the first time.
  • DIY builders. Fill planters and level ground for a patio base with one measured order.

Tips for Best Results

  • Measure at the widest points: curves and slopes hide extra square feet.
  • Keep the depth honest: a new lawn needs 4 to 6 inches, flower beds 6 to 8, raised beds 8 to 12.
  • Grade away from the house: finished soil should sit a few inches below the siding.
  • Add 10 percent for waste and settling: loose soil compacts after watering, so the level drops.
  • Plan for the wet weight: a cubic yard of wet topsoil can weigh up to about 2,700 lb.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do i calculate how much topsoil i need?

Multiply length x width x (depth / 12) to get cubic feet, then divide by 27 for cubic yards. That is how to calculate amount of topsoil needed for any bed.

How to calculate topsoil needed for a raised bed?

Measure the inside length and width and the full soil depth, usually 8 to 12 inches. A 4 by 8 foot bed at 12 inches deep needs 1.19 cubic yards.

How much does one cubic yard of topsoil weigh?

About 2,100 lb dry, or about 1.05 tons. After heavy rain the same yard can weigh up to about 2,700 lb.

How many bags of topsoil are in a cubic yard?

A cubic yard holds 27 bags of 1 cubic foot, or 36 bags of 0.75 cubic feet. Pick your bag size in the calculator.
