How to Use Calcotia's Sod Calculator

  1. Measure the length and width of the lawn in feet. For an irregular lawn, split it into rectangles and add them up.
  2. Enter the two numbers. The calculator works out the area in square feet.
  3. Pick your pallet size: most pallets cover 450 square feet, and some cover 400 or 500.
  4. Set the waste allowance; 10 percent covers most lawns.
  5. Click Calculate and read your square feet, pallets, rolls and pieces. Add a price per pallet to see the cost.

Why Use This Tool

  • Pallets, not just square feet. Suppliers sell sod by the pallet, so this sod calculator divides your lawn by the pallet size and rounds up.
  • How to calculate how much sod i need without coming up short: add 10 percent for cutting around curves, paths and trees.
  • Rolls and pieces counted. Big rolls cover about 10 square feet each, and slabs are 16 by 24 inches, about 2.67 square feet each.
  • Cost at a glance. A sod installation cost calculator must show money, not just area. Enter your price per pallet for the material cost.
  • Honest numbers. Pallet coverage is not standardised, so pick 400, 450 or 500 square feet and confirm the figure with your supplier.

Who Can Use It

  • Homeowners. Price a new lawn before you call the sod farm.
  • Landscapers. Quote a job on site with pallets and material cost in one pass.
  • Gardeners. Work out how to calculate sod for a small patch or a full front lawn.
  • DIY builders. Lay your own lawn with the right number of pallets delivered on the day.

Tips for Best Results

  • Measure twice, at the widest points, and add rectangles for curves and corners.
  • Use 5 percent waste for a plain rectangle, 10 percent for a typical lawn, 15 percent for curves and slopes.
  • Order whole pallets: the calculator rounds up, because no supplier sells half a pallet.
  • Lay sod the day it arrives: it dries out fast on the pallet, so have the soil ready.
  • Water deeply right after laying, and keep it damp for two weeks while roots take hold.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many square feet does a pallet of sod cover?

Common sizes are 400, 450 and 500 square feet per pallet, with 450 the most common. Large-roll pallets can cover 500 to 700. Confirm with your supplier.

How to calculate sod for an irregular lawn?

Split the lawn into rectangles, work out each as length x width, and add the areas. Then add 10 to 15 percent for waste.

How much does sod cost per pallet?

A 500 square foot pallet often costs roughly $150 to $380 for materials only. Enter your supplier's price per pallet to see your material cost.

How soon should I lay sod after delivery?

The same day, if you can. Sod dries out and heats up on the pallet within hours. Prepare the soil first, then order for laying day.
