Why Use This Tool

  • A coffee ratio calculator. 55 grams per litre, about 1:18; most brewers work between 1:15 and 1:18. Start at 1:16.
  • A coffee to water ratio calculator. Drip 1:16, pour over 1:16, french press 1:15.
  • A french press coffee calculator. The dose for a full press: 4 minute steep, coarse grind.
  • A how much coffee for 12 cups calculator. Twelve cups need about 178 grams at 1:16, roughly 25 tablespoons.
  • Grams, not scoops. Weighing beats scoops: a gram is the same no matter the roast or grind.

Who Can Use It

  • Home brewers. If your morning pot tastes different every day, a fixed ratio fixes it.
  • French press fans. The press is forgiving, but the right dose still matters most.
  • Anyone scaling a recipe. Doubling a pour over or brewing for guests is simple with a fixed ratio.

Tips for Best Results

  • Weigh your water too. How do you calculate coffee ratios by hand? Divide water grams by 15, 16 or 18.
  • Grind for your method. Coarse for french press, medium for drip, medium-fine for pour over.
  • Use water at 195 to 205°F (90 to 96°C). Boiling water scorches it; cooler water under-extracts it.
  • Check your 12 cup math. As a how much coffee grounds for 12 cups calculator, this tool gives about 178 grams at 1:16.
  • Use it as a how much coffee for french press calculator. A 32 oz press holds about 950 ml and needs about 63 grams at 1:15.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the golden ratio for coffee?

One gram of coffee to 15 to 18 grams of water, from the Specialty Coffee Association Golden Cup Standard: 55 grams per litre, about 1:18. A 1:15 ratio tastes stronger, 1:18 lighter, 1:16 is the middle.

How much coffee do I need for 12 cups?

Twelve 8 oz cups hold about 2.8 litres. At 1:16 that needs about 178 grams; at 1:15 about 190, at 1:18 about 158. If a cup is 5 oz, 12 cups needs about 111 grams at 1:16.

What ratio should I use for french press?

Use 1:15 with a coarse grind and a 4 minute steep. A stronger ratio and coarse grind keep the cup sweet instead of muddy or bitter.

Should I measure coffee in grams or tablespoons?

Grams, whenever you can. A tablespoon weighs about 7 grams, but scoops vary. A cheap kitchen scale is the best upgrade for consistent coffee.

Does the ratio change for pour over?

Pour over usually uses 1:16, the same as drip, with a medium-fine grind. Sour: more coffee or a finer grind. Bitter: less coffee or a coarser grind.
