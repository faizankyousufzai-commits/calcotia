ACT Score Calculator: Find Your Composite Score

Last updated 2026-11-09 · By Faizan Khan Yousufzai

Our free ACT score calculator turns your section scores into your ACT composite score from 1 to 36. Enter English, Math, and Reading, add Science if you took it, and the tool averages them the way ACT does. It works for the enhanced ACT and the classic ACT, so pick the format you took.

On the enhanced ACT, the composite is the average of English, Math, and Reading. Science is optional and does not count toward the composite. On the classic ACT, all four sections count.

How to Use Calcotia's ACT Score Calculator

  1. Pick your format: enhanced ACT if you tested in 2025 or later, classic ACT if you tested earlier.
  2. Enter your English, Math, and Reading section scores, each from 1 to 36.
  3. Add your Science score if you took it. It feeds your STEM score, not your enhanced composite.
  4. Click Calculate to see your composite score.

How to calculate ACT score results: add the section scores, divide by the number of sections, and round to the nearest whole number. An average ending in .5 rounds up. This ACT test score calculator does it for you.

Why Use This Tool

  • Both formats covered. This enhanced ACT score calculator handles the current three-section composite, and it also handles the classic four-section composite.
  • STEM score included. If you took Science, you also get your STEM score, the average of Math and Science.
  • Exact rounding. The tool rounds exactly the way ACT does, so an average of 29.5 becomes 30, never 29.

Who Can Use It

  • Students planning a retake. See how many points one stronger section adds to your composite before you book another test date.
  • Students comparing formats. If you have old and new scores, this act composite score calculator shows both on the same 1 to 36 scale.

Tips for Best Results

  • Use the right format. Mixing a classic four-section average with an enhanced three-section result gives a wrong answer.
  • Remember each section counts equally. On the enhanced ACT, one section is a third of your composite, so a 3-point gain in one section adds 1 point overall.
  • Check the STEM score if you are applying to science or engineering programs, since some colleges look at it closely.
  • Compare your composite with the middle 50 percent range of your target colleges.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are ACT scores calculated?

ACT counts correct answers for a raw score, with no penalty for wrong answers. Each raw score converts to a scaled score from 1 to 36 by equating, then the section scores are averaged into your composite.

How do you calculate ACT score composite?

On the enhanced ACT, add English, Math, and Reading, divide by 3, and round to the nearest whole number. On the classic ACT, add all four sections and divide by 4. For example, 28, 30, 32, and 26 average to 29.

How ACT composite score is calculated on the enhanced ACT?

On the enhanced ACT, Science is optional and does not count. The composite is the average of English, Math, and Reading only. If you took Science, ACT reports it separately and combines it with Math into a STEM score.

What is a good ACT composite score?

A composite of 21 is around the national average. A 24 or higher is above average, 28 or higher is strong, and 33 or higher puts you near the top. Compare your score with the range your target colleges publish.
