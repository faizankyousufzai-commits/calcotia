Solar Panel Calculator: How Many Panels Do You Need?

Last updated 2026-11-09 · By Faizan Khan Yousufzai

Our free solar panel calculator turns your monthly electricity use into a panel count, a system size, and an estimated installed cost. Enter your power use, your peak sun hours, and the panel wattage you are considering. It is a PV solar panel calculator and a solar panel cost calculator in one.

How to Use Calcotia's Solar Panel Calculator

  1. Enter your monthly use in kWh, from your power bill.
  2. Enter your peak sun hours, usually 3.5 to 7 a day.
  3. Pick a panel wattage. Most home panels in 2026 are 400 to 460 watts.
  4. Enter the cost per watt from a quote, or keep the $2.60 default, then click Calculate.

Why Use This Tool

  • A panel count, not a guess. It divides your daily use by what one panel makes.
  • System size in kW. Installers quote by system size, so your kW makes quotes easy to compare.
  • An honest cost estimate. It uses your cost per watt, so it matches local prices, not a national average.
  • No outdated tax credit. The 30% federal residential credit ended for systems completed after December 31, 2025.
  • Battery and inverter ready. See below for how to calculate solar panel battery and inverter needs from your daily use.

Who Can Use It

  • Homeowners getting quotes. Check an installer's panel count against this calculator before you sign.
  • Buyers comparing panel sizes. See how 400-watt panels compare with 430-watt panels for your roof.
  • Renters and planners. Estimate what a system would look like, even before you own a roof.
  • Anyone sizing storage. Your daily kWh from this tool is the starting point for battery sizing.

Tips for Best Results

  • Use a full year of bills. Summer cooling and winter heating make one month misleading.
  • Check your roof's shade and direction. A shaded or north-facing roof makes less power.
  • Get three installer quotes and compare them by cost per watt, not total price.
  • Ask each installer how many peak sun hours they assumed.
  • A solar panel installation cost calculator quote from an installer adds roof work, permits, and wiring.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many solar panels do I need?

A home using 899 kWh a month with 4.5 peak sun hours needs about 20 panels of 400 watts, an 8 kW system.

How to calculate solar panel needs for home?

Divide monthly kWh by 30.4 for daily kWh, then divide by peak sun hours times 0.86, allowing for 14% losses. That gives system size in kW; divide by one panel's wattage for the panel count.

How to calculate solar panels needed for home with a battery?

First size the panels from your daily use. Then size the battery from the power you use after sunset, usually 30 to 50% of daily use.

How to calculate how many solar panels I need for a smaller roof?

Start with your roof space. A 400 to 450-watt panel is about 1.7 m by 1.1 m, so allow about 2 square metres per panel. Twelve panels of 430 watts make a 5.2 kW system.

Is the 30% federal solar tax credit still available in 2026?

No. The 30% residential clean energy credit ended for homeowner systems completed after December 31, 2025. Systems finished by that date can still claim it, but a system installed in 2026 gets no federal residential credit.
