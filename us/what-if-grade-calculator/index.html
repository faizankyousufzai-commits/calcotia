How to Use the What If Grade Calculator

  1. List each category, such as homework, quizzes, and exams. Add its weight.
  2. Enter the scores you have earned so far.
  3. Leave unfinished work blank, or type a what-if score.
  4. Set your target grade, such as 90%.
  5. Click Calculate to see your current grade and the score you need.

Why Use This Tool

  • Weighted math done for you. This grade calculator with weights handles categories worth different percentages.
  • Target mode. See the exact score needed on the next exam to reach your goal.
  • Instant what-ifs. Change one number and watch your grade update.
  • Final exam planning. Use it as a grading calculator final tool to learn if a goal is still realistic.
  • Clear results. Calculate grade percentages in one view, with no formulas or spreadsheets.

Who Can Use It

  • High school and college students. Check if an A is still possible before finals week.
  • Seniors. Use it like a grad calculator to see what you need to pass a class required for graduation.
  • Parents and tutors. Turn "How are we doing?" into a clear, numbers-based plan.
  • Students near a cutoff. See whether one strong exam can lift a B+ to an A-.

Tips for Best Results

  • Copy category weights from your syllabus.
  • Include every graded item, even small ones. Missing categories skew the result.
  • Test three what-if scores: worst case, likely case, best case.
  • Example: you have 88% on work worth 75% of your grade. To end at 90%, you need 96% on the final worth 25%.
  • Ask your teacher about dropped scores, extra credit, and rounding. A calculator can't see them.
  • Update the numbers after each new grade.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does a what if grade calculator work?

It multiplies each score by its weight and adds the results. For a target, it works backward: it finds the score needed on remaining work to reach your goal. The math is simple, but your teacher's grading rules may change the final result.

How do I calculate a weighted grade by hand?

Multiply each category's average by its weight. Add the results. If your weights total 100%, that sum is your grade. If they total less, divide the sum by the total weight used so far.

How to calculate GPA from my class grades?

Multiply each course's grade points by its credit hours. Add those results, then divide by total credit hours. On a common 4.0 scale, A is 4, B is 3, and C is 2. Schools may weight honors courses differently.

Can I use it to see if I can still pass?

Yes. Enter a passing target, such as your class's passing mark, and see the score you need on remaining work. If the result is over 100%, that goal is out of reach unless extra credit exists.

About the math

This calculator uses standard weighted-average arithmetic: each score is multiplied by its weight, the results are added, and the total is divided by the weight used so far. There is no official source for this math, because it is a general arithmetic method, not a rule from any authority.
