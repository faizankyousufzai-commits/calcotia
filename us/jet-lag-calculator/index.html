Jet Lag Calculator: Recovery Days and a Sleep Plan

Last updated 2026-11-09 · By Faizan Khan Yousufzai

Our free jet lag calculator estimates how many days it takes to adjust after a flight across time zones. Enter time zones crossed, direction, usual bedtime and arrival time, and get an estimated recovery time plus a sleep-shift plan. The rule is simple: about one day per time zone east, half a day west.

How to Use Calcotia's Jet Lag Calculator

  1. Enter time zones crossed, from 1 to 12: usually the hour difference between home and destination.
  2. Choose east or west. Flying east is harder: you must fall asleep earlier than your body wants.
  3. Enter your usual bedtime and arrival time.
  4. Click Calculate to see recovery days, a bedtime-shift plan, and a first-day nap tip.

Why Use This Tool

  • A jet lag recovery calculator you can plan around. Six zones east means about six days, while six zones west means about three.
  • Direction built in. Eastbound asks your body clock to move earlier, which most find harder. Westbound asks it to move later, which is usually easier.
  • A sleep plan, not just a number. Bedtime shifts one hour a day for up to three nights before you fly.
  • Answers the when to sleep question. This when to sleep jet lag calculator shows which nights to move bedtime, and whether a short nap on arrival day helps or hurts.

Who Can Use It

  • Long-haul flyers. Crossing five or more time zones is when jet lag bites hardest.
  • Business travelers. Shifting sleep before the flight beats recovering after it.
  • Families flying with children. Kids adjust on the same clock rules, and a shared plan keeps the trip calmer.

Tips for Best Results

  • Start shifting before you fly. Move bedtime one hour earlier nightly for eastbound trips, one hour later for westbound.
  • Use light as your strongest cue. Morning light helps after flying east; evening light helps after flying west.
  • Use this how to avoid jet lag calculator before you fly. Shifting sleep early and landing with a light plan beats any cure after arrival.
  • Melatonin needs care. Low-dose melatonin timed to destination bedtime helps some travelers; ask a doctor first. A melatonin jet lag calculator cannot set your dose.
  • Nap short or not at all. Morning arrival: a 20 to 30 minute nap is fine. Afternoon arrival: skip the nap.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to calculate jet lag recovery time?

Count time zones crossed, then multiply: about one day per zone east, half a day per zone west. Six zones east means about six days, six zones west about three.

Why is flying east worse than flying west?

Your body clock runs slightly longer than 24 hours, so staying up later comes naturally, while falling asleep earlier fights your rhythm.

When should I sleep after a long flight?

Sleep at local bedtime on arrival night, even if tired earlier. A short morning nap is fine; avoid long daytime naps.

Should I use melatonin for jet lag?

Low-dose melatonin a few hours before destination bedtime helps some travelers shift earlier, especially flying east. Timing matters more than dose, so ask a doctor first.

Can I avoid jet lag completely?

No, but you can shrink it. Shift sleep before the flight, get the right light, stay hydrated, and keep the first day low-key.
