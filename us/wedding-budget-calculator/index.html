How to Use Calcotia's Wedding Budget Calculator

  1. Enter your total wedding budget, the full amount you plan to spend.
  2. Enter your guest count, including yourselves and the wedding party.
  3. Click Calculate to see each category in dollars and cost per guest.
  4. Move money between categories: the percentages are a starting point.

Why Use This Tool

  • Real percentages, not guesses. Venue 29%, catering 24%, photo and video 10%, flowers 9%: The Knot 2026 Real Weddings Study.
  • Per-guest math included. See how to calculate wedding budget based on guest count: divide each category by your guest list.
  • A wedding flower budget calculator built in. The flowers and decor line gives your flower budget in dollars.
  • A wedding floral budget calculator for the same line. Floral design and decor share one budget, as planners keep them.
  • Free to use, no signup. Run as many budget and guest-count combinations as you like.

Who Can Use It

  • Couples setting their first budget. If you have a total but no idea how it splits, this is the fastest way to see it.
  • Couples comparing guest counts. Cutting 20 guests saves real money on catering.
  • Anyone checking a vendor quote. Compare a photographer's price with the 10% photo and video share.

Tips for Best Results

  • Book venue and catering first. Together they take 53% of a typical budget, and set your date.
  • Learn how to calculate a wedding budget by hand. Multiply your total by each percentage: 29% of $30,000 is $8,700 for the venue.
  • Plan for a smaller list early. Fixed costs like photography stay flat, so per-guest cost rises as the list shrinks.
  • Work out drinks inside catering. For how to calculate alcohol budget for wedding receptions, ask your caterer for a per-head bar price and multiply by guests.
  • Keep a buffer outside the total. Set aside 5% extra for surprises, so one unexpected cost does not break the plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the average wedding cost?

The Knot 2026 Real Weddings Study surveyed 10,474 US couples married in 2025 and put the average wedding at $34,200. Treat it as a benchmark: costs vary by city, season and guest count.

What percentage of a wedding budget goes to the venue?

Venue and rentals take about 29%, catering, cake and drinks another 24%. Together 53%, which is why those two decisions shape everything else.

How do I calculate a wedding budget for 100 guests?

Divide your total by 100 for the per-guest cost, then split each category by percentage. On $30,000 for 100 guests, that is $300 per guest, with $8,700 for venue and $7,200 for catering.

How much should flowers cost at a wedding?

Flowers and decor usually take about 9%, and The Knot puts the average flower spend near $2,800. In-season blooms and reusing ceremony flowers cut that line most.

Should I keep a contingency fund?

Yes. Keep about 5% aside, outside the category splits, for extra guests and overtime charges.
