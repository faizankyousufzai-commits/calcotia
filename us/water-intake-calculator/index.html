Water Intake Calculator: How Much Water Should You Drink?

Last updated 2026-11-09 · By Faizan Khan Yousufzai

Our free water intake calculator turns your body weight, activity level, and climate into a daily water target in ounces, litres, and cups. It starts from the half-your-body-weight rule, then adds water for exercise and hot weather. It is a daily water intake calculator for anyone who wants a personal number instead of the old eight-glasses rule.

How to Use Calcotia's Water Intake Calculator

  1. Enter your weight in pounds or kilograms.
  2. Pick your activity level: sedentary, lightly active, moderately active, or very active.
  3. Pick your climate: normal or hot and humid.
  4. Click Calculate.
  5. Read your daily target in fluid ounces, litres, and cups, and spread it through the day.

Why Use This Tool

  • A personal number. Your target comes from your weight, not a one-size-fits-all rule.
  • Activity counts. Exercise adds water: light activity adds 12 oz, moderate adds 24 oz, and very active days add 36 oz.
  • Climate counts. Hot and humid weather adds 12 oz, because you sweat more.
  • Three units. Results show fluid ounces, litres, and cups, so the number works however you measure.
  • Grounded in real guidance. The National Academies set an adequate total water intake of about 3.7 litres a day for men and 2.7 litres for women, including water from food and all drinks.

Who Can Use It

  • Gym goers and runners. Replace what you sweat out instead of guessing.
  • Office workers. A clear daily target makes it easier to keep a bottle at your desk and finish it.
  • People in hot climates. Heat raises your needs, and this tool adds for it.
  • Anyone tracking health. Use the number as a daily goal alongside sleep and steps.

Tips for Best Results

  • Spread your water through the day. Drinking it all at once is less useful.
  • Remember that food counts. About a fifth of your total water comes from food.
  • Drink more on workout days, even if the calculator already adds for activity.
  • Thirst is a late signal, so drink on a schedule during long workouts or hot days.
  • Talk to a doctor first if a health condition limits your fluids. This tool is for healthy adults.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to calculate water intake by weight?

Divide your weight in pounds by two: a 150-pound person starts at 75 oz a day. This water calculator intake method then adds water for activity and heat.

How to calculate water intake for exercise days?

Start with half your body weight in ounces, then add 12 oz for light activity, 24 oz for moderate, and 36 oz for very active days. Add 12 oz more in hot and humid weather.

How to calculate daily water intake from food and drinks?

Total water includes all drinks plus the water in food. The National Academies' adequate intake is 3.7 litres a day for men and 2.7 litres for women, but about 20% comes from food.

How to calculate required water intake if I drink coffee or tea?

Coffee and tea count toward your fluid intake, since they are mostly water. They are mild diuretics, but they still hydrate you. Count them, and keep plain water as the biggest share.

How much water should I drink a day?

For most healthy adults, the half-body-weight rule gives a good target: 75 oz for a 150-pound person, 100 oz for a 200-pound person, before activity and heat additions.
